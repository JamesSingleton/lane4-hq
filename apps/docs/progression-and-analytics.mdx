# Follow progression and team trends

Progression is one swimmer's times. Analytics is the team view: volume, attendance, top times, and the cut tracker.

<video controls src="/videos/progression.mp4" width="100%" />

## Progression

Times stay with the team that recorded them. Choose the season, then the swimmer, then the event. The chart plots that event across the meets you have already brought in. Lower on the chart is a faster swim. You can also import a CSV of times when you do not have a full results file.

## Analytics

Analytics reads practices and results you already entered. The top row is recent yardage and the attendance rate. Team top times lists the fastest swim on file for each event. The cut tracker, farther down the page, lists swimmers already at or under a standard you saved.
