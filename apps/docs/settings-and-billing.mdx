# Manage settings and billing

Settings are split between the team and your personal account.

## Team settings

Use the team settings area to manage the team profile, members, billing, and team-specific integrations. Only owners and authorized coaches should make changes that affect access, billing, or data imports.

## Your account

Open **Settings → Account** to update your profile and account security. Contact support if you need to change the email address associated with the account.

**Active sessions** lists every browser and computer signed in to your account, including <a href="/desktop-app">Lane4 Meet Manager</a>. End a session to sign that device out.

## Billing

Open **Settings → Billing** to review the plan and manage the team’s subscription. Features such as additional coach seats, lineup support, relay suggestions, and advanced analytics depend on the plan.

## Connected services

The available integrations vary by team type. Reauthorize a provider when its connection expires, then confirm the team data is still connected before relying on an automated sync.
