# Manage settings and billing

Settings splits team administration from your own account. Team profile and coach invites are covered in [team setup](/team-setup). This page is the rest: your account, the plan, and connections that depend on team type.

## Your account

Open Settings, then Account, to update your profile, appearance, and security. Contact support if the email on the account needs to change.

Active sessions lists every browser and computer signed in, including Lane4 Meet Manager. End a session to sign that device out.

## Billing

Open Settings, then Billing, to review the plan and the subscription. Extra coach seats, lineup tools, relay suggestions, and advanced analytics depend on the plan. Only an owner should change billing.

## Connected services

What you can connect depends on the team type. Club teams can use USA Swimming tools. High-school teams do not show those settings. When a connection expires, sign in to the provider again and confirm the team data is still the team you expect before you trust an automatic sync.
