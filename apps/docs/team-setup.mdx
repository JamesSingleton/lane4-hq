# Set up your team

Open Settings in the sidebar. Team holds the name and address that show up on entries. Members is where you invite coaches.

<video controls src="/videos/team-setup.mp4" width="100%" />

## Team profile

Use Settings, then Team, to keep the name, abbreviation, LSC, and mailing address current. The abbreviation is what Meet Manager entry packs use. For a high-school team, class year and school eligibility are the roster fields that matter. Club teams use USA Swimming fields instead. Lane4 only shows the settings that fit the team type you chose.

## Members and roles

Invite each coach from Settings, then Members. Give people the least access they need:

- Owner manages the team and billing.
- Head coach runs the coaching workflow.
- Other roles help the staff without every administrative permission.
