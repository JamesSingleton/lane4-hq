# Run your team's meet workflow

Lane4 HQ is where the visiting team prepares its roster, entries, and results. It does not host the meet, merge other teams' entry files, or replace a timing console. The host runs the meet on deck in Lane4 Meet Manager.

<video controls src="/videos/meets.mp4" width="100%" />

## Create or import a meet

From Meets, choose Add meet or Import meet file. An event file can bring in sessions, events, and qualifying times. The meets table lists the season, course, location, and opponent for each meet you already have.

## Work through the meet

Open a meet and use its tabs:

- Information for the name, date, course, and location.
- Events for the program.
- Entries for individuals and relays.
- Reports for a coach printout.
- Results, from the meet header or the meets section, after the meet is swum.

<Note>
  Lane4 HQ supports swim events. Diving events are skipped so a swim-only team can keep working with the file.
</Note>
