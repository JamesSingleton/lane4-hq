# Get started with Lane4 HQ

Sign in with the email on your coach account. The password field hides what you type. After sign-in, Lane4 opens the team you belong to.

## Create your team

A new account goes through onboarding before the dashboard:

1. Sign up or sign in.
2. Enter the team name and select club or high school. The type changes roster fields and meet rules, so pick the one this team actually is.
3. Finish onboarding. The dashboard opens for that team.

You can belong to more than one team. Use the team switcher in the sidebar to move between them without signing out.

## Start with the essentials

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set up the roster">
    Add swimmers and coaches, create groups, or import a roster file.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite your coaching staff">
    Add members from Settings, then Members, and assign a role.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the next meet or practice">
    Put the next practice or meet on the calendar. You do not need a full season entered before the rest of the app is useful.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Keep club and high-school teams separate. Roster data, meets, and progression stay with the team you have open.
</Tip>

The dashboard shows the active roster, recent attendance, the next meet, and best times dropped this season.
