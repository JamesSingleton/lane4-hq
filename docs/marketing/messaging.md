# Lane4 HQ marketing messages

Copy for `apps/web` follows this brief. Claims below are limited to what the coach workspace does today.

## Audience

Club coaches and high school coaches, equally. Many people coach both. Speak to a Saturday invitational and a Tuesday dual in the same breath. College fields exist in the product; college is not a third audience on the site.

## The grind

Name the work, then show the feature that takes it off the desk:

- Retyping seed times from a printout, last season's Team Manager, or a spreadsheet the night entries are due.
- Juggling Hy-Tek Team Manager, a spreadsheet, and a whiteboard that do not agree on who is going.
- Chasing who is entered, and who already has too many events.
- Pasting Sunday's results into Excel to see who dropped time.
- Keeping JO, sectionals, or a high school cut in your head.
- Building the individual lineup and the relays the night before the meet.

Do not invent a number of hours saved. The time saved is the job that goes away.

## Positioning

Lane4 HQ is the coach's workspace for the team they take to someone else's meet. Best times fill the seeds. The lineup is the file you export. Sunday's results come back next to previous best and a cut you entered. One login covers a club and a high school; each team keeps its own roster, entries, and times.

## Key messages

1. **Seeds fill from best times, so the printout stays in the drawer.** The swimmer's best time fills the seed. The coach can override it before export. This is on Free.
2. **The lineup is the meet, in the order it is swum, with limits checked before you export.** Individuals and relays, scratches that keep the row, four relay legs plus alternates. Entry limits from the file cover individual, relay, and combined events. High school adds association scoring caps: max names in an individual event, and max relay teams. Export HY3 or CL2 for Meet Manager, or SDIF when the host asked for it. A host can still reject a file. Print individual entries and a split sheet; the default print omits alternates, and split cadence is print-only.
3. **Sunday's file comes back next to previous best, and next to a cut the coach entered.** Import HY3, CL2, SDIF, or a ZIP. Faster swims update best times. Unmatched rows stay visible. Time standards are sets the coach creates or imports. Any plan can turn one set on for a single meet. The roster-wide cut list is Pro.
4. **A club and a high school stay separate on one login.** Switch teams without signing out. High school stores class year (freshman through senior), JV and Varsity from the host file, and association limits. A USA Swimming ID is not required. A club roster can store a USA Swimming ID. SafeSport training gates minor contact and medical fields, and the dashboard says so only when a check is missing.

## What a paid plan adds

Free is one coach, unlimited swimmers and meets, import and export, seeds, progression, high school limits, and 5 practice drafts or relay-order suggestions a month. Pro is up to 5 coaches, lineup suggestions that fill leftover individual spots from eligible best times (relays stay as staffed), the roster-wide cut list, and 20 drafts a month, then overage. Enterprise is unlimited coaches and 100 drafts a month, then overage. Do not publish an overage price. Relay-order suggestions and practice drafts share one monthly pool on every plan. Lineup suggestions are Pro only, and they are not a full lineup.

## Voice

Write the sentence a coach would repeat to another coach at a meet. Second person. Full sentences. Name the artifact: printout, Team Manager, spreadsheet, whiteboard, HY3, Excel, JO, sectionals, dual, invitational. Lead with the outcome, then the feature that produces it.

Avoid parallel triplets, fragment headlines, and lines that are clever on their own. Do not open a page by listing what the product refuses to be.

## Leave out

- The line "We don't run the meet," and any cousin of it ("we don't run the pool").
- Meet hosting, merging other clubs' entries as a product, running the pool, a timing console, or the desktop meet manager.
- SWIMS roster sync. Lane4 HQ is not an approved USA Swimming API vendor, so the site must not say it can sync a real club's SWIMS roster. Storing a USA Swimming ID is allowed. The not-certified statement stays in the FAQ, terms, and privacy only.
- A built-in JO or sectionals book, per-day entry caps, a parent portal, and dues.
- A guarantee that every host import succeeds.
